No two small businesses are exactly alike. One owner is drowning in paperwork. Another can't keep up with payroll. A third has a great product but no real system behind the scenes — and things are starting to slip. The common thread? They all need help, but they all need *different* help.

That's exactly why we built B2B Solutions the way we did.

## We Work on the Problems You Haven't Had Time to Solve

Right now, we're working with several small businesses on special projects — the kind of work that keeps getting pushed to the back burner because the day-to-day never stops.

Streamlining operations. Cleaning up disorganized systems. Building processes that actually hold up when things get busy. This is the kind of behind-the-scenes work that doesn't show up in a product or a storefront, but makes everything else run better.

If any of that sounds familiar, you're not alone.

## The Services That Make It Happen

Here's a look at what we help with most:

### Bookkeeping
Accurate, up-to-date books aren't just for tax season — they're how you actually know how your business is doing. We keep your records clean so you always have a clear picture of where you stand.

### Payroll Administration
We don't run payroll in-house — but we do handle the administration side so it's one less thing on your plate. Through our network affiliate [Cascade Payroll](https://www.cascade-payroll.com), we connect you with reliable payroll processing and manage the coordination so everything runs smoothly.

### Administrative Support
The emails, the scheduling, the documents, the follow-ups — it adds up fast. We take the administrative load off your plate so you can focus on the work only you can do.

### Creative Problem Solving
Not every challenge fits neatly into a category. Sometimes a business just needs someone to look at a problem with fresh eyes and figure out what will actually work. That's something we genuinely enjoy.

## Finding the Right Fit

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all packages. When you reach out to us, we take the time to understand what's actually going on in your business — what's working, what isn't, and what would make the biggest difference. Then we build a plan around that.

Whether you need ongoing support or help with a specific project, we're flexible enough to meet you where you are.
